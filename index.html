01 / About

Philosophy

I design systems that scale — not just screens that ship. Every component, every token, every workflow is a decision about how a team will work tomorrow.

Over the past nine years I moved from graphic design at VNExpress — Vietnam's largest newsroom — through cloud UI at Bizfly, product design at iCheck, and design leadership at Meey Land. Today I lead product design at CMC Cyber Security, where I'm responsible for the UX of cybersecurity tooling used by enterprise clients across Vietnam.

My work sits at the intersection of three disciplines: systems thinking, data-heavy interfaces, and GenAI-augmented workflow — using AI to compress research, ideation, and variant generation without sacrificing craft.

I lead small design teams the same way I lead a component library: clear ownership, documented decisions, and a bias toward shipping over perfecting.
